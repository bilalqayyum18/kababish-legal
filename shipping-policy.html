Delivery Policy

Last Updated: January 31, 2026

This Delivery Policy explains how food orders are delivered when placed through the Kababish Restaurant application.

1. Delivery Area

Kababish Restaurant currently provides delivery services within selected areas of Rawalpindi & Islamabad, Pakistan.

2. Delivery Time

Estimated delivery times typically range between 30 to 60 minutes depending on order volume, traffic conditions, and distance.

3. Delivery Delays

Delivery times may occasionally be affected by:

  • Traffic congestion
  • Weather conditions
  • High order volume
  • Operational issues

We will make reasonable efforts to deliver orders as quickly as possible.

4. Delivery Charges

Delivery charges may vary depending on distance, peak traffic hours, weather conditions and may be displayed during checkout in the application.

5. Failed Deliveries

If the delivery driver is unable to reach the customer due to incorrect address or unavailable contact information, the order may be cancelled.
